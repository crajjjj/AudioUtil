what is an MFG expression preset?
One preset = one full face: 33 values, applied in-game through MfgFix's MfgConsoleFuncExt.ApplyExpressionPreset (SLO VE stores them as 0–100 in StorageUtil jsons like FemaleExpressions.json).
[0–15] phonemes — the mouth: Aah, BigAah, BMP (lips pressed), ChJSh, DST, Eee, Eh, FV, I, K, N, Oh, OohQ, R, Th, W.
[16–29] modifiers — BlinkL/R, BrowDownL/R, BrowInL/R, BrowUpL/R, LookDown/Left/Right/Up, SquintL/R. Holding Blink* or Look* non-zero stops the actor's natural blinking in-game.
[30] expression — the base mood worn under everything: 0–6 Dialogue (Anger, Fear, Happy, Sad, Surprise, Puzzled, Disgusted), 7 MoodNeutral, 8–14 Mood (same seven), 15 CombatAnger, 16 CombatShout (opens the mouth like a phoneme — avoid without good reason).
[31] strength of that expression — 0 means dynamic, the mod picks a value at apply time. [32] speed — SLO VE's transition speed for the apply.
N/A on a channel = leave it untouched when applying — how override presets (e.g. blowjobphonemeoverride) change only part of the face.
Also supported: ConditionalExpressionsExtended profiles (SKSE\Plugins\CondiExp\Expression_<name>.json) — a floatList of female1–7 / male1–7 phases picked by emotion intensity, 32 floats scaled 0–1, no speed value. Drop one in to edit it; the editor converts scales automatically. Format docs: expression-json.
